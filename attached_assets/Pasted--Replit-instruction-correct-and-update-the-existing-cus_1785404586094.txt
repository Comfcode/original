## Remove or correct inaccurate content

Delete or rewrite the following statements currently found in the article.

### 1. Perfume

Remove:

* “200 мл духов сверх нормы”;
* “духи — 50 мл”;
* “туалетная вода — 250 мл”.

There is no separate modern EU millilitre allowance for perfume or eau de toilette. Perfume is included in the general value allowance for other goods.

### 2. Fuel in portable cans

Remove the statement suggesting that a traveller entering Poland from Belarus may automatically carry 10 litres of Belarusian fuel in a portable can.

The 10-litre Polish rule is a generic allowance for otherwise permitted fuel arriving from a third country. It must not be presented as overriding EU sanctions concerning Belarus-origin or Belarus-exported petroleum products.

For Lithuania and Latvia, clearly state that Belarus fuel must not be carried in an ordinary portable can. Fuel in the vehicle’s standard tank may be treated differently and remains subject to conditions and customs assessment.

### 3. Tobacco

Remove the wording:

> Табачные изделия сверх установленного лимита запрещены.

Replace it with wording explaining that:

* the figures of 40 cigarettes, 20 cigarillos, 10 cigars or 50 grams of smoking tobacco are ordinary duty-free ceilings;
* they do not legalise a product whose import is prohibited by sanctions;
* Belarus-origin or Belarus-exported cigarettes, cigarillos and cigars are prohibited under the applicable EU sanctions;
* smoking tobacco must be assessed separately according to its product classification, origin and the rules of the country of entry;
* Lithuania has a broader national prohibition covering Belarus-origin tobacco and manufactured tobacco substitutes.

### 4. Alcohol

Explain that:

* 1 litre above 22% and 2 litres up to 22% are alternative allowances;
* proportional combinations are allowed within 100% of that allowance;
* 4 litres of still wine and 16 litres of beer are additional allowances;
* the allowances only apply when the product itself is not prohibited;
* spirit drinks under CN 2208 originating in or exported from Belarus are prohibited under EU sanctions;
* Lithuania’s national prohibition is broader and includes Belarus-origin alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages;
* Latvia states that its Belarus sanctions prohibition applies to spirit drinks, while naturally fermented wine and beer are excluded from that specific prohibition.

### 5. Electronics

Remove the unsupported statement that electronics above €750 automatically require a written declaration.

Do not publish a separate €750 electronics threshold unless a current official customs source explicitly supports it.

Explain instead that new electronics, gifts and other purchases normally count toward the general value allowance and that customs may assess whether goods are for personal or commercial use.

### 6. Trips shorter than 24 hours

Remove the general statement:

> Сниженные лимиты применяются при поездках короче 24 часов.

Do not use journey duration as a universal EU rule.

Explain that reduced or special limits may instead depend on:

* transport-crew status;
* regular international transport;
* border-worker or border-area-resident status;
* frequency of crossing;
* national customs rules.

### 7. Independent limits

Delete:

> Каждая категория — независимая норма.

Replace it with the following:

> **Важно:** указанные нормы действуют только для разрешённых товаров некоммерческого характера и не отменяют санкционные запреты. Табачные изделия — 40 сигарет, 20 сигарилл, 10 сигар или 50 г табака — являются альтернативными нормами. Допускается пропорциональное сочетание в пределах 100% общей нормы.
>
> Для алкоголя 1 л напитков крепостью свыше 22% или 2 л напитков крепостью до 22% также являются альтернативной нормой и могут пропорционально сочетаться. Дополнительно разрешаются 4 л тихого вина и 16 л пива, если соответствующий товар не подпадает под санкционный или национальный запрет.

Ordinary EU rules confirm the lower land-travel tobacco limits, proportional combinations, the alcohol structure, the €300 general allowance and the absence of a separate perfume allowance.
